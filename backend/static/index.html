GradeSense AI Quick Tutorial

Learn how to monitor, simulate, and prevent quality deviations on the paper machine.

1 Triggering Transitions

Use the controls in the top bar to select a **Target Grade** and a **Mode**. Click Trigger Grade Change to start.

Choosing Steam Lag or Excess Stock Ramp will inject physical anomalies to test the predictive advisory system.

2 Predictions & SHAP

The **Overview** and **AI Predictions** tabs show a circular risk gauge. If the risk of Basis Weight exceeding the **±2.5%** limit rises, the gauge pulses red.

Real-time SHAP analysis attributes the risk score to specific parameters (e.g. Steam Pressure) with a plain-language explanation.

3 Accepting Recommendations

Under **AI Advisory**, the copilot recommends quantified, actionable setpoint adjustments (e.g., Reduce Stock Flow by 3.5%).

Click Accept Setpoint to approve the recommendation or Reject if it's not useful. Operator feedback is stored for quality evaluation.

4 Scenario Planning (What-if)

Open the **What-if Simulator** to virtually adjust process parameters (Stock Flow, Steam Pressure, Machine Speed) before changing actual plant setpoints.

The simulator re-calculates predicted Basis Weight and risk in real-time so you can plan the safest recovery path.
